# Loader skull model

- Model: Skull, by Vladimir Petkovic (2025)
- Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/ScatteringSkull
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- Loader file: `scattering-skull.glb`
- Optimization: quantized geometry and WebP texture; mesh simplification disabled

The public Meshy model “Realistic Human Skull Anatomy” did not expose a direct GLB download from its public page. This verified, intact CC0 skull is used as the available local alternative.
